roomtone: logo options

"Room tone" is what a sound engineer records when everyone goes quiet: the sound of the room itself. Every mark here keeps the brand rules: hard edges, ink on paper, and the red dot as the only circle. The underlined Fraunces wordmark carries over unchanged.

My pick

A. Floor plan

A room drawn the way an architect draws one, doorway and all, with the tone inside. It's the "interior designer for sound" idea in one shape, and the open door says "come back." It's the only option that couldn't belong to any other business.

Option

B. The room

Same idea without the door: a closed square with the tone in the middle. The boldest at 16px, but a dot in a box is a common shape, so it's the least ownable.

Option

C. Reflections

The tone, and the sound coming back off the walls. It's the most literal about acoustics. The cost is that it gets busy as a favicon, and it can read as a target.

Option

D. Keep the stamp

The current dot over a bar still makes sense: a tone sitting above the floor. It's the zero-work option, but the bar was really an underscore, and that story is gone.
